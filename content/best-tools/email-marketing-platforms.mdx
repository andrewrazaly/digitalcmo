## How we ranked these platforms

We scored each tool on automation depth, deliverability controls, ecommerce fit, pricing clarity, and how well it supports a content → email → revenue loop—the same loop Digital CMO uses to monetise traffic.

## 1. ActiveCampaign — best automation for service businesses

ActiveCampaign shines when nurture sequences, CRM-lite fields, and conditional logic matter more than a pretty template gallery.

**Best for:** agencies, coaches, B2B lead funnels  
**Watch-out:** interface density; plan carefully before migrating

## 2. Klaviyo — best for ecommerce revenue

If your blog or store sells products, Klaviyo’s behavioural flows (browse abandon, post-purchase, win-back) usually outperform generic newsletter tools.

**Best for:** DTC brands and content sites with a store  
**Watch-out:** cost scales with contacts and sends

## 3. Kit (ConvertKit) — best for creators and newsletter brands

Kit keeps creator workflows simple: forms, sequences, recommendations, and clean subscriber tagging without enterprise bloat.

**Best for:** solo publishers and newsletter-led brands  
**Watch-out:** less ideal for complex multi-brand ecommerce

## 4. Brevo — best budget all-rounder

Brevo’s free tier and SMS add-ons make it attractive when you’re validating monetisation before committing to a premium ESP.

**Best for:** early-stage blogs and lean teams  
**Watch-out:** advanced automation trails ActiveCampaign

## 5. Mailchimp — best familiar starter

Mailchimp remains the default many teams already know. Solid templates and journeys; pricing can climb quickly as lists grow.

**Best for:** teams wanting familiar UI and quick launches  
**Watch-out:** evaluate cost at your projected list size

## FAQ

### What email platform is best for affiliate blogs?
Start with Kit or Brevo. Move to ActiveCampaign when you need deeper behavioural automation.

### How often should a marketing blog send email?
Weekly is enough for most niches. Consistency beats volume.

### Do I need SMS too?
Only after email conversion rates are stable. SMS is expensive noise if your offer is unclear.
