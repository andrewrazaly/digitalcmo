## Overview

Choosing a CRM is a significant decision for Australian small businesses. This guide walks you through the key considerations.

## Step 1: Define Your Needs

List the features you need: contact management, pipeline tracking, email integration, reporting. Avoid overbuying.

## Step 2: Consider Your Budget

CRMs range from free (HubSpot, Zoho) to hundreds per month (Salesforce). Start small and scale.

## Step 3: Test Before Committing

Most CRMs offer free trials. Use them to test with real data and workflows.

## Step 4: Check Australian Support

Ensure the vendor supports Australian businesses—time zones, currency, and local compliance matter.

## Recommended Tools

- **HubSpot** – Free tier, good for inbound
- **Zoho CRM** – Affordable, full-featured
- **Pipedrive** – Sales-focused, simple

## FAQ

### Do I need a CRM as a sole trader?

It depends. If you have more than a handful of leads, a CRM helps. Free options like HubSpot make it low-risk.

### What about GST and Australian compliance?

Most CRMs don't handle GST directly—that's for your accounting software. But they should integrate with Xero, MYOB, etc.
