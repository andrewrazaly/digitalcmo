## Overview

Buffer is a social scheduling tool built for teams that want a clean calendar without enterprise pricing. For many digital marketing blogs and small agencies, it’s the right “good enough” layer between manual posting and heavy platforms like Sprout Social.

## Pricing

Buffer’s free tier is useful for testing. Paid Essentials plans start around $6 USD per channel per month—affordable when you’re validating a content distribution habit.

## What Buffer does well

- Fast scheduling across major networks
- Simple analytics that don’t overwhelm
- Browser extension for saving posts on the go
- Clear pricing versus seat-heavy enterprise tools

## Where it falls short

- Limited social listening
- Weaker advanced approval workflows for large agencies
- Not a full community management suite

## Who should buy Buffer

Choose Buffer if you’re a creator, affiliate publisher, or lean marketing team using social mainly to distribute articles and capture attention.

Choose Hootsuite or Sprout Social if you need deep listening, heavy client reporting, or large multi-brand workflows.

## FAQ

### Is Buffer good for agency client work?
Yes for scheduling-focused retainers. For listening-led community management, look higher upmarket.

### Can Buffer replace a full social suite?
Not for enterprise. For distribution-led marketing blogs, it’s often enough.

### Does Buffer help monetisation directly?
Indirectly—by keeping distribution consistent so SEO content reaches more sessions and email signups.
